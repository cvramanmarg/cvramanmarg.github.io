Barapullah Phase III opens today, with Ring Road near Maharani Bagh expected slow until 2 pm

Barapullah Phase III elevated corridor inaugurated

Union Home Minister Amit Shah inaugurated the PWD's Barapullah Phase III elevated corridor today, a signal-free link between Mayur Vihar Phase I and Sarai Kale Khan. Reports put the cost at about Rs 1,635 crore and describe six lanes, a cycle track, pedestrian sidewalks and an extra-dosed bridge over the Yamuna. The foundation stone was laid in 2014 and construction began in April 2015 against a 30-month target. Reports cite land acquisition, power-line relocation, the 2018 Yamuna flooding and tree-cutting permissions among the causes of delay. Combined with Phases I and II, the run is described as roughly 9 to 10 km of signal-free road toward AIIMS.

NEEDS CHECK: length is 3.5 km in two outlets, 4.9 km with ramps in another; time savings are quoted differently (Mayur Vihar to AIIMS from 35-40 minutes to about 15, versus 20-25 minutes saved at peak)
Sources: Daily Jagran, Sunday Guardian, India.com, 29 Sep 2026

Ring Road advisory, 9 am to 2 pm today

Delhi Traffic Police advised motorists to avoid the Ring Road stretch between Sarai Kale Khan and Ashram Chowk from 9 am to 2 pm today because of the PWD's inauguration event for the Barapullah corridor. Drivers were asked to use alternative routes and follow instructions from traffic personnel at the site. Sarai Kale Khan, Maharani Bagh and Ashram Chowk were named as the affected areas, and slow-moving traffic was expected on key routes around them through the day. The published reports did not list specific diversions.

Sources: ThePrint, 28 Sep 2026; Daily Jagran, 29 Sep 2026

Planned BSES power cuts listed for New Friends Colony and Taimoor Nagar

A Times Now Navbharat schedule lists two planned power cuts for transformer preventive maintenance in the area. The first is a one-hour cut, 10 to 11 am today, listed under "New Friends Colony", with Gafoor Nagar, Okhla, shown as the affected area. The second is from 11 am to 12 noon on 30 September at Block B, Taimoor Nagar. The schedule gives maintenance as the only reason and advises residents to check with their power company for exact details of their neighbourhood.
